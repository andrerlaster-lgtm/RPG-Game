Paper Thief

House Veyrane steals with paper. Tonight, ZAY steals it back.

Somewhere in the Veyrane counting house sits the contract choking The Crooked Halo. Slip into the estate, take the ledger, and get to the river boat.

Stay out of the lantern light. If a guard catches you, it's a fight: Hit or Steal when ZAY's gauge fills. The guild's code still holds: no casualties. Guards only get knocked out.
